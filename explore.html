WaqtX treats Islamic knowledge with honesty. Every entry is tagged with its evidence type and certainty level — so you always know the basis of what you are reading.

Quran Direct verse references. Primary source. Takes precedence in all religious matters.
Hadith Collection + chapter + hadith number. Grading (sahih/hasan) noted where relevant.
Classical Ibn Hisham, al-Tabari, Ibn Sa'd, al-Baladhuri, Ibn Kathir and other major traditional sources.
Academic Modern historical scholarship. Valuable for context and corroboration — distinct from religious authority.
Disputed Where different accounts exist, we say so — rather than presenting one version as unquestionable.

Honest acknowledgment of what is established vs. debated is a sign of scholarly integrity — a value central to the Islamic tradition of ‘ilm (knowledge).
